This repository is the public artifact of a larger private system. The boundary is deliberate:

The point of the boundary is not secrecy for its own sake — it is that the engineering discipline is the portfolio piece, and it can be documented honestly and completely without exposing the system it governs.

## What it is

A fully autonomous trading system that researches, validates, and executes strategies across **24 registered symbols** — precious metals (gold, silver, platinum, palladium, copper), energy (crude and refined products), equity indices (German and Japanese benchmarks), and major & Canadian-dollar forex pairs — across a multi-timeframe range spanning 5-minute to daily bars, with live execution seated on the hourly and 4-hour engines, quoted end-to-end in the broker's native instrument symbols via MetaTrader 5. The execution bridge is broker-agnostic by design: any MetaTrader 5 terminal can sit on the execution side.

The system runs end-to-end without human intervention: market data ingestion, signal generation across **ten strategy families**, an ML specialist layer, risk management with hard circuit breakers, order execution through a MetaTrader 5 bridge, a live monitoring dashboard, and automated alerting. It currently operates on a demo account by design: promotion to live capital is gated on clean out-of-sample evidence, under governance the system was specifically built to enforce. That discipline is the point of the engineering.

## Engineering highlights

- **40,000+ lines of Python**, one cohesive system: strategy engines, a walk-forward tuning pipeline, a bridge service connecting Linux (WSL2) to a Windows-hosted MetaTrader 5 terminal, a real-time dashboard, and a watchdog supervisor.
- **1,700+ automated tests**, with CI fully green on the current build — the full pytest suite, the strict type gate, and the 65% coverage floor all passing. The suite is hermetic by construction: a corpus-presence guard makes every data-dependent test declare itself and skip explicitly where the corpus is absent, and a mechanised pre-push gate runs the touched modules' tests plus the linter locally before any branch leaves the machine — so local and CI verdicts can no longer silently diverge. The type-checking budget is enforced in CI as a ratchet: when CI revealed the true type-error count was higher than local runs had measured, the difference was root-caused and the debt was paid down to the original ceiling, file by file. The ceiling was never raised.
- **A single shared market-data layer, proven equivalent before it went live.** All market data — a stored corpus of 426 files spanning 39 symbols × 11 timeframes (2018–present, including five years of 5-minute bars for every symbol) — is now read through one accessor backed by a sha256 checksum manifest. The repoint onto it was behavior-preserving by construction and by proof: output hashes captured before the change match the new layer's output exactly, across a 148-capture equivalence sweep of every served symbol, with zero discrepancies.
- **A book that announces itself.** At every startup the engines publish a machine-readable manifest of exactly which strategy arms loaded and which registry rows were skipped, with the gate that stopped each one named. A watchdog check compares the live book against the registry continuously and raises a critical alert on any unexplained absence — a detection, logging, and delivery chain proven end-to-end by live drills, including a deliberately planted phantom registry row.
- **Repeated independent full-system audits** (October 2026) spanning the execution bridge, risk layer, strategy engines, research pipeline, data layer, ML layers, and repository hygiene — the latest, graded October 29, 2026 under an expanded rubric that added runtime-activation evidence and the structured-logging standard, stands at **82/100 (B)**, with every material finding remediated or formally dispositioned and the corrections graded by the operator rather than self-certified.
- **An idea foundry for strategy generation (October 2026; second programme completed November 2026).** A generation system mints strategy candidates from a finite grammar — 97,680 configurations in its second-generation form, across the ten families — every candidate given a permanent, never-reused name *before* testing, charged to the trial ledger at generation, and archived permanently whatever its result, so the graveyard is as auditable as the book. Generation runs under hard programme caps (200 per batch, 2,000 per programme), so the multiple-testing charge is bounded by design, and nothing the foundry produces trades without a protocol PASS and an operator seating decision. **Programme #1** completed under a pre-registration committed before generation: 2,000 candidates generated, 1,971 measured, and **zero holdout budget consumed** — no candidate reached the sealed segment, so nothing was seated. A forensic autopsy root-caused three generation-machinery defects (duplicate candidates minted by the generator, a collapse of family coverage, and a recording cap on the performance metric) — each fixed, with the fixes enforced mechanically in the second programme's design rather than left to vigilance. **Programme #2** completed in November 2026 on the widened grammar: 1,949 candidates measured, 171 carried through to the sealed holdout — and **zero certified**, the strongest of them falling short of the programme's multiplicity-corrected bar. That result is reported here exactly as it is reported internally: a funnel that has measured 3,920 candidates across two programmes and promoted none of them is not a funnel that failed; it is a funnel whose *yes* will mean something.
- **A machine-checked engine registry, and engines that report their own silence.** Every timeframe engine is inventoried in a declarative registry — module, order-routing identity, routed symbols, live status — verified against the code by a checker that exits PASS. The census behind it (October 31, 2026) fixed the desk's ground truth: H1 live with seven arms, H4 live with four, the 15-minute engine evaluated-and-gated, the 5-minute engine absent by design until it ships. When the 4-hour engine then produced a zero-activity window, the audit trail led to an entry guard comparing live state against a frontier it could silently fall behind. The fix (November 2026) is now a standing observability layer in both live engines — a **frontier guard**: the entry frontier is recomputed from the current data on every poll, every evaluated non-entry emits a reason-coded missed-entry event, any divergence between the simulation's state and the live frontier is detected and logged with both timestamps, and consecutive divergences escalate to a critical operator alert. A quiet engine is now an observable engine, and an engine that disagrees with itself says so out loud. The 15-minute engine's machinery was separately certified sound — 99 evaluated would-be entries correctly suppressed by its closed gate in a four-day window — while its legacy validation was rejected on provenance and a sealed re-validation specified in its place (trial budget 500, family-wise α ≈ 1×10⁻⁴).
- **A higher-timeframe context gate, running in shadow (November 2026).** A Layer-1 permission gate in the top-down stack: for every would-be entry on the live engines, it reads the daily timeframe's trend state (two independent reads, reported separately) and its RSI stretch context, under strict as-of discipline — only daily bars that closed *before* the entry bar opened are visible to it, so a forming bar can never leak into a decision. It is wired into both live engines in shadow mode only: it logs the verdict it *would* have returned for every real signal and changes no trade, no size, and no exit. It was built before any trial was charged against it, deliberately — when it is eventually measured, the question will be whether the trades it would have vetoed were systematically the losers, answered from evidence it is accumulating now.
- **A dashboard that shows the trading, not just the state (November 2026).** The operations dashboard gained a trade chart — candlesticks for any registered symbol and timeframe, with entry markers, exit markers carrying each closed trade's result, and open positions drawn with their entry, stop, and take-profit lines — over a merged live event feed that renders every supervisor and engine event stream in one reverse-chronological view, including event types the dashboard has never seen before, rendered generically rather than dropped. Before it was trusted, the chart was verified deal-for-deal against the broker-side ledger on the live book: every in-window deal matched its marker, with zero unmatched.
- **ML train/serve parity as a tested invariant.** The feature assembly that trains the expected-R model and the assembly that serves it live were proven to read the same bars — measured skew 0.0 across 127 live entries — and six hermetic tests now guard that parity permanently. Parity is an invariant with a test suite, not an assumption.
- **Sealed validation protocol** — SELECT → VALIDATE → HOLDOUT with fail-closed governance: a holdout segment is spent exactly once, every tuning trial is written to an auditable ledger (**122,405 charged runs**), and family-wise significance is charged (Šidák correction) before any result may be called evidence.
- **A full independent audit cycle** (four phases) that found the original out-of-sample numbers were contaminated by selection leakage — then rebuilt the measurement pipeline so the leak is structurally impossible, and re-measured the entire book before trusting a single figure.
- **Supervision and hardening, second generation.** The desk runs as systemd user services with linger enabled (tracker, watchdog, dashboard), each with supervised restarts; the tracker and watchdog now also supervise *each other* — a dedicated thread in the tracker watches the watchdog's heartbeat, a failure mode found by drill, fixed, and then proven by a second drill in which the watchdog was deliberately stopped and the tracker's alert fired and cleared as designed. On the Windows side, a dedicated Bridge Guardian scheduled task keeps the MetaTrader 5 bridge alive, relaunching it within 10 seconds of a crash — a recovery proven by a live kill drill — and the terminal itself now autostarts at logon. Bridge authentication was rebuilt so the token lives only in its own gitignored file, with health verified by fingerprint, and later hardened to fail closed at import rather than run unauthenticated. A startup trap that re-archived trade history on every launch was found, fixed with a single-pass batch rewrite, and proven cured by a clean cold start after a full Windows reboot; Windows auto-restart has been disarmed, with a documented recovery path in its place. Every live path now emits structured, timestamped JSON logs under a single standard.
- **Repository discipline.** Feature branches land through consolidation sittings behind a secrets scan and per-merge CI gates, with desk invariants (bridge fingerprint, registry hash, arm count) proven identical before and after — most recently three builds in a single day (the frontier guard, the dashboard upgrade, the shadow context gate), each merged only after independent code review of the branch against its report. The main branch, the running desk, and the documented state are the same code, verified by commit hash.
- **Reliability and security engineering**: a tracker-freeze root-cause investigation traced intermittent hangs to watchdog restart logic interacting with blocking I/O; the fix (isolated worker, hard timeouts, fail-fast reads) shipped with proof tests. A configuration audit caught a subsystem that could have routed orders outside its mandate; it was disabled and verified — the trade journal proved zero orders had ever been placed through it. A later audit found a secrets file tracked in version control; it was untracked and every live credential in it was rotated, with the running system verified healthy afterward.
- **Risk as code**: hard breakers on peak-equity drawdown, daily loss, and consecutive losses; per-trade and portfolio risk caps; a news-event blackout calendar wired into every engine; position sizes tiered by evidence quality — with the effective breaker levels (25% peak-equity drawdown, 10% daily loss, 8 consecutive losses) formally ratified as an operator decision after an audit caught documentation drift on exactly those numbers.

## The live demo book

The system currently runs an **eleven-arm book autonomously on a MetaTrader 5 demo account** — every arm below was confirmed loaded and cycling by name in the live startup manifest at the most recent supervised restart (November 3, 2026), across a market-data feed covering 24 symbols:

- **H1** — GOLD trend · SILVER trend · PLATINUM trend · USDJPY mean reversion · USOIL breakout · HEATOIL trend · LSGOIL trend
- **H4** — CADCHF trend · GER40 trend · JPN225 trend · CADNOK trend *(seated, currently disabled pending re-analysis)*

Seats carry the protocol's verdict labels in the registry — certified, unstable, probe, and paper-live seats are distinct classes, sized accordingly, and seats that exist to accumulate forward evidence are labelled as such and capped at reduced size. The book is not static: in November 2026 GOLD's seat changed families, its range-grid configuration swapped for the trend configuration through a gated merge-and-restart sitting — a seating decision executed with the same ceremony as a code change, because under this governance it is one. No arm trades on an untested parameter set.

## The validation story

When the clean protocol re-measured the book, the results reset the expectations set by the old, leaky pipeline — which is exactly what the rebuild was for. On the clean board, GOLD trend and SILVER trend hold pass-class verdicts on never-before-seen data, and CADCHF validated in the 4-hour programme and now trades at paper-live status while it accumulates forward evidence. Just as important is what the protocol refuses. Across the foundry's two completed programmes, 3,920 candidates were measured and 171 were carried to the sealed holdout — and not one cleared it at its multiplicity-corrected bar, so not one was promoted on foundry evidence. The book continues to run on demo while candidates accumulate forward evidence. Promotion under the project's governance requires clearing a multiple-comparisons significance charge as well as the protocol's own bar — no candidate gets promoted on a single measurement.

That is the discipline this project demonstrates: measurement infrastructure rigorous enough to be trusted when it says *yes* — and believed when it says *no*.

## The full package — front end to back end

This is a full-stack system, designed and built end-to-end by one engineer:

- **Front end** — a real-time operations dashboard (NiceGUI web UI): live positions, equity, per-strategy-arm state, risk-breaker status, alerting — and, since November 2026, a trade chart with entry/exit markers and a merged live event feed — served locally by design. It is the operator's window into everything below it.
- **Middle layer** — an execution bridge API (FastAPI, token-authenticated REST with idempotent order endpoints) connecting the Linux compute environment (WSL2) to a Windows-hosted MetaTrader 5 terminal (demo account), with an independent account-guard check on the compute side.
- **Back end** — the strategy engines, the walk-forward research pipeline with its schema-versioned trial ledger, the risk layer with hard circuit breakers, a SQLite trade journal, and a heartbeat-monitored watchdog supervisor.

## Architecture at a glance

- **Strategy engines** on four timeframe tracks, all inventoried in a machine-checked engine registry — the H1 engine (primary, live) and the H4 engine (live) run the book today; a 15-minute engine is scaffolded with its new-entries gate disabled — its machinery audit-certified, its sealed re-validation specified — pending certification; a 5-minute engine is in design, its data layer already complete — hosting ten families — trend, breakout, mean-reversion, range grid, session breakout, liquidity sweep, Donchian, swing, and statistical arbitrage
- **Frontier-guard observability** inside both live engines: the entry frontier recomputed every poll, reason-coded missed-entry events, simulation-vs-live divergence detection with escalation alerting — silence in an engine is now a measured state, not an accident
- **Market-data layer**: one shared accessor over a checksummed corpus (426 files, 39 symbols × 11 timeframes, 2018–present) with a sha256 manifest — every consumer reads through the same door, and the layer's equivalence to the parsers it replaced is hash-proven
- **Walk-forward tuner** with a sealed segment pipeline and a schema-versioned trial ledger
- **Idea foundry**: governed candidate generation over a finite grammar (97,680 configurations in its current generation) — named before testing, ledger-charged at generation, failures archived permanently — feeding the same sealed funnel as any hand-written hypothesis; two programmes complete, 3,920 candidates measured, zero certified
- **Validation stack** (in build): an adoption-placebo harness that runs the full funnel on randomized entries to calibrate the next programme's VALIDATE-stage significance threshold; purged, embargoed cross-validation diagnostics; cost-stress ladders — diagnostics that inform the scorecard and never veto; certification remains the sealed holdout's alone
- **Higher-timeframe context gate** (shadow mode): daily trend reads plus RSI stretch context under strict as-of discipline, logging would-be vetoes on live signals; changes no trade while it accumulates the evidence that will govern its own measurement
- **ML layer** (gradient-boosted models): an expected-R model (LightGBM; XGBoost and CatBoost are maintained in the same evaluation bench) serves in an observer role informing position sizing, and a specialist layer — one bundle set per strategy family — runs in permanent shadow mode after a dedicated audit of its training-data provenance, per-family expectancy, and sizing impact, scoring and logging only while it accumulates the evidence base that will govern any future retrain decision
- **Execution bridge** between the Linux compute environment and MetaTrader 5, with token authentication held in a dedicated gitignored file, fingerprint-verified health checks, and an independent account-guard check on the compute side
- **Supervision**: systemd user services with linger (tracker, watchdog, dashboard) under supervised restarts, a Windows Bridge Guardian that relaunches the MT5 bridge within 10 seconds of a crash, heartbeat monitoring with failure classification, bot-based alerting, and a local operations dashboard
- **Research stack** (isolated environment): indicator-parity certification against TA-Lib, vectorized-backtest reconciliation, and seeded, replayable hyperparameter studies

## Roadmap and current status

- **Phase 1 — Research and validation infrastructure.** Complete: the sealed SELECT → VALIDATE → HOLDOUT protocol, the auditable trial ledger, and the audit cycle that rebuilt the measurement pipeline after selection leakage was found in it.
- **Phase 2 — Autonomous demo deployment.** Live: the eleven-arm book runs unattended on a MetaTrader 5 demo account under the supervision stack described above.
- **Phase 3 — Evidence programmes.** Programmes #1 and #2 are complete (3,920 candidates measured, 171 reaching the sealed holdout, zero certified). **Programme #3 is in preparation** under redesigned geometry — programme cap 500, certification bar |t| ≈ 3.88, a holdout-sample eligibility floor (n ≥ 400), and the cost floor gating at VALIDATE rather than merely recorded. The validation stack (adoption placebo, purged/embargoed CV diagnostics, cost-stress ladders) is being built first, so the programme's thresholds are calibrated on randomized noise before they are frozen at pre-registration. Programme #2's 171 near-miss candidates are paper-tracked forward — process counts only, no interim results — to a single fixed read point in May 2027. A sealed re-validation of the 15-minute engine remains specified.
- **Phase 4 — Operational hardening and platform evolution.** Ongoing: the frontier guard, the trade-chart dashboard, and the shadow context gate are live (November 2026). Next in build order: an ML scorer bake-off — LightGBM defending against a random-forest challenger and a mandatory linear floor, on identical features, labels, and purged time-series splits — and a registry/loader re-architecture that seats multiple strategies per symbol under a per-symbol combined risk cap. The full unattended logon-recovery drill remains the last formal proof on the hardening list.
